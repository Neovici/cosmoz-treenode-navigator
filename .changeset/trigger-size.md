`cosmoz-treenode-button-view` accepts a `size` attribute (`sm` | `md` | `lg` | `xl`) and passes it to its trigger button. When the container is too narrow for the label, the trigger no longer insists on a 50px minimum and centres its icon instead, so it fits an icon-only sidebar rail.
